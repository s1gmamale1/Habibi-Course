## Guidance for future lesson openers

Openers are a privilege, not a default — use one **only** where a real
milestone justifies it, keep it under 10 spoken minutes, and draw **only**
from the verified items in this file (or a future file that has gone through
the same live-verification process).

- **Lesson 1.10 (vowels/harakat milestone)**: item #5 (Tirmidhi 2910, reward
  per letter, Alif-Lam-Mim) — gives immediate, tangible stakes to precise
  pronunciation of each vowel/letter.
- **Lessons 2.13 / 2.14 (first real Fatihah reading)**: items #7 (Bukhari
  756 / Muslim 394a, no prayer without al-Fatihah) and #8 (Abu Dawud 832,
  the accommodation/"filled his hand" hadith) — land the obligation
  encouragingly, right at the moment the obligation becomes personally
  relevant.
- **Lesson 3.1 (first tajweed rule)**: item #2 (Muslim 798a, double reward
  for the struggler) — the course's thesis hadith, reintroduced exactly when
  tajweed rules start feeling hard.
- **Hifz / memorization milestones**: item #4 (Muslim 804a, the Qur'an as
  intercessor on the Day of Resurrection) — long-term, eternal-weight framing
  for sustained memorization effort.
- **Lesson 2.10 (first real words milestone)**: item #19 (triliteral root
  fact, ك ت ب) revisited as a "look how far you've come" callback, optionally
  paired with item #3 (Muslim 2699a, path to knowledge) to frame vocabulary
  growth as part of the broader virtue of seeking knowledge.

No other lesson in the current syllabus has a milestone weighty enough to
warrant a dedicated opener; default to a plain warm-up for all other lessons.
