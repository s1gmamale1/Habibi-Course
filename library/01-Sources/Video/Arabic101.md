## Three structural habits worth copying

1. **Every stage ends with revision and/or a Q&A session.** Four of the five
   stages have an explicit Q&A day. Consolidation is scheduled, not left to the
   student.
2. **Madd comes first**, before noon sakina — the opposite of most classical
   syllabi, which open with noon sakina/tanween (as [[Tuhfat-al-Atfal]] does).
   His stated reason: natural madd is "the foundation of many types of madd to
   come", and you are "NOT allowed to recite withOUT paying attention to your
   natural madd." It is the one rule a learner cannot avoid from the first ayah.
3. **Ghunnah is introduced subordinately**, inside Stage Two session three
   ("idgham with ghunna — we'll also learn what ghunnah is"), rather than as its
   own topic. This vault does **not** follow him here — see the pedagogical fix
   in [[Tuhfat-al-Atfal]] — but the divergence is deliberate.

## Tajweed Common Mistakes — From Good to Better (30 videos)

A numbered remediation series (#1–#28 plus an intro and an adhan bonus),
originally Ramadan 2022. Positioned *after* the rules are known — error

## The ordering philosophy, in one line

> Symbols and stopping before sounds; madd before nasals; nasals before
> qalqala; qalqala before heaviness; articulation as a parallel track; mistakes
> as a separate course; rare rules and rule-conflict resolution last.

## Retrieval method and confidence

All titles, video IDs, durations, playlist IDs and channel statistics were
parsed from YouTube's own `ytInitialData` JSON in the server-rendered HTML, and
the stage/session labels from `videoDetails.shortDescription` on each watch
page. **Nothing in the tables above is reconstructed from memory.** The "Topic"
columns for the Advanced playlist are interpretations of retrieved titles and
descriptions, and are labelled as such.

**Not retrieved:** per-video upload dates, per-video view counts, transcripts
and captions, the contents of 10 non-tajweed playlists, and video counts for 12
of the 22 playlists.
