# Al-Idghām ash-Shafawī — الإِدْغَام الشَّفَوِيّ

## Definition

**Merging a meem sākinah into a following meem**, producing one doubled meem carried on a
**2-count ghunnah**.

Also called **إِدْغَام المُتَمَاثِلَيْنِ الصَّغِير** (*idghām al-mutamāthilayn aṣ-ṣaghīr*)
or **إِدْغَام مِثْلَيْه** — because it is simply the general rule for two identical letters
meeting, applied to the meem. See [[Idgham-Mutamathilayn]] for that theory.

## Condition

Meem sākinah (مْ) followed by **م**.

## Type — always kāmil

**Idghām kāmil**, always. Two meems become one doubled meem; nothing of the first survives
except that its duration is now part of the shaddah. There is no nāqiṣ variant and no
disagreement.

The muṣḥaf shows it plainly: in `أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن` the first meem is bare and the second
carries a **shaddah**. Two written meems, one pronounced letter.

## Duration

**2 counts of ghunnah**, on the doubled meem. See [[Ghunnah]] — this is level 2 on the
ghunnah scale, the same length as the mushaddad meem of `حَمَّالَةَ` (111:4), because it
*is* a mushaddad meem; it just happens to have been assembled from two words.

## Inside the memorised sūrahs — the gap that isn't

Course research recorded idghām shafawī as having **no clean example inside the ḥifẓ set**
(al-Fātiḥa + sūrahs 105–114). **Checked against the pinned corpus, that is wrong.**

**Sūrat Quraysh 106:4 contains two of them:**

> ٱلَّذِىٓ أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعٍ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ خَوْفٍۭ

- **`أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن`** — the meem of `هُم` merges into the meem of `مِّن`
- **`وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ`** — the same, again

Both are corpus-verified. This means the ayah already flagged as the course's **contrast
anchor** for [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]] vs [[Izhar-Halqi]] is in fact carrying **four** rules:

| Phrase | Rule |
|---|---|
| `أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن` | **Idghām shafawī** (this rule) |
| `مِّن جُوعٍ` | [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]] — ج |
| `وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنْ` | **Idghām shafawī** |
| `مِّنْ خَوْفٍۭ` | [[Izhar-Halqi]] — خ |
| `خَوْفٍۭ` (on continuation) | [[Iqlab]] — into `بِسْمِ` of al-Māʿūn |

Five rulings in one ayah of eight words, every one of them inside a sūrah the learner
already recites. **106:4 should carry the whole nasal syllabus.**

## The trap — tanwīn + meem is a different rule

The candidate reference `عَلَيْهِم مِّنْ` at **2:5** does **not** exist; 2:5 is
`أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ عَلَىٰ هُدًى مِّن رَّبِّهِمْ وَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلْمُفْلِحُونَ`. What it
actually contains is `هُدًى مِّن` — a **tanwīn** before م, which is
[[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah|idghām bi-ghunnah]] (a *noon* merging), not idghām shafawī (a *meem*
merging).

This confusion is worth teaching deliberately, because on the page the two look identical:
a bare word ending, then a meem with a shaddah.

| What precedes the shaddah'd meem | Rule | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| A bare **meem** — `أَطْعَمَهُم مِّن` | **Idghām shafawī** | `#58B800` |
| A **tanwīn** — `هُدًى مِّن`, `بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن` | [[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah]] | `#169777` |
| A bare **noon** — `مِّن مَّسَدٍۭ` | [[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah]] | `#169777` |

The 2-count ghunnah is the same in all three; only the classification and the colour
differ. A learner will not mispronounce this — but they will mis-*name* it, and the naming
is what the exam and the colour-coded muṣḥaf test.

`حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍۭ` (111:5) puts rows two and three side by side.

## Common mistakes

- **Pronouncing two separate meems** — `hum-min` instead of `humm`.
- **Omitting the ghunnah** on the merged meem.
- **Failing to hold the shaddah**, releasing after one count.
- **Calling a tanwīn-plus-meem "idghām shafawī"** — see the table above.
