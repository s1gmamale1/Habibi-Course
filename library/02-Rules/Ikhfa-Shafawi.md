# Al-Ikhfāʾ ash-Shafawī — الإِخْفَاء الشَّفَوِيّ

## Definition

**Concealing the meem sākinah** with a **2-count ghunnah** when it is followed by **ب**.

*Shafawī* means "labial" — of the lips. It is named against
[[Ikhfa-Haqiqi|al-ikhfāʾ al-ḥaqīqī]], the "true" concealment, which hides a noon.

## Condition

Meem sākinah (مْ) followed by **ب**.

In the Qurʾān this **always happens across two words** — the sequence مْ + ب does not occur
inside a single word. So every instance is a word-boundary case, and the meem is always the
last letter of its word.

## Articulation — the light touch

The lips **touch lightly, without pressure, throughout the ghunnah**. They do not seal.

If the lips press shut you have produced a fully-formed meem — [[Izhar-Shafawi|iẓhār
shafawī]], the wrong rule. If they stay apart, you have produced no meem at all.

This is the same lip posture as [[Iqlab]], and the resulting sound is nearly
indistinguishable. The rules differ in **origin, not in output**:

| | Written letter | Becomes | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Iqlab]] | **ن** / tanwīn before ب | a concealed meem | `#26BFFD` |
| **Ikhfāʾ shafawī** | **م** before ب | a concealed meem | `#D500B7` |

Both end at the same place. One of them travelled there.

## Duration

**2 counts of ghunnah**, held while the lips are lightly closed, before the ب is released.
See [[Ghunnah]].

## Scholars differ — ikhfāʾ or iẓhār?

A **minority** of scholars hold that a meem sākinah before ب is **iẓhār**, not ikhfāʾ: the
meem is simply pronounced, since it and the ب already share the lips and nothing needs
hiding.

**The jumhūr position is ikhfāʾ, and this course teaches ikhfāʾ.** Flag the minority view
once so a learner who hears a teacher press the lips fully on `تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ`
recognises a known difference rather than an error — but do not drill it.

Practically the two positions differ by very little: the disagreement is over how much lip
contact and how much nasal hold, not over whether a meem is there.

## Inside the memorised sūrahs

**`تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ` — al-Fīl 105:4** is the single instance in the ḥifẓ set, and it
is a good one: the meem is the last letter of `تَرْمِيهِم`, the ب opens `بِحِجَارَةٍ`, and
three words later the same ayah gives an [[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah|idghām bi-ghunnah]] on
`بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن`. One ayah, two lip-and-nasal rules.

## Common mistakes

- **Pressing the lips shut** — the default English/Turkic habit, and it converts the rule
  to iẓhār.
- **Leaving the lips apart**, so no meem sounds at all.
- **Omitting the ghunnah** and releasing straight into the ب.
- **Confusing it with [[Iqlab]]** — both sound alike; only the written letter tells you
  which rule you are in.
