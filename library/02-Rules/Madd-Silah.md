# Madd aṣ-Ṣilah — مد الصلة

## Definition

The **[[Ha-soft|hāʾ]] aḍ-ḍamīr** — the third-person masculine singular pronoun suffix
ـهُ / ـهِ — is given a small madd when it stands **between two vowelled
letters**. An implied **[[Waw|wāw]]** is heard after a ḍammah, an implied **[[Ya|yāʾ]]** after a
kasrah.

The mushaf prints this implied letter as a **small wāw** (ـهُۥ) or a **small
yāʾ** (ـهِۦ). That superscript letter *is* the rule, made visible.

## Condition

The hāʾ aḍ-ḍamīr must be:
1. preceded by a **mutaḥarrik** (vowelled) letter, **and**
2. followed by a **mutaḥarrik** letter,
3. **in wasl only** — the ṣilah drops entirely at [[Waqf-Types|waqf]].

## The two sizes

| | Follows | Length | Behaves like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ṣughrā** (صغرى, minor) | any vowelled non-hamzah letter | **2** | [[Madd-Tabii]] |
| **Kubrā** (كبرى, major) | a **hamzat qaṭʿ** | **4–5** | [[Madd-Munfasil]] |

Ṣilah kubrā *is* a munfaṣil: the implied wāw/yāʾ ends the word and a hamzah
begins the next. It therefore inherits munfaṣil's whole ruling, including the
**2-count option via ṭarīq aṭ-Ṭayyibah**. Keep it equal to your munfaṣil count.

## Where the rule does *not* apply

| Word | Why no ṣilah |
|---|---|
| مِنْهُ | preceded by a **sākin** nūn |
| فِيهِ | preceded by a madd yāʾ |
| إِلَيْهِ | preceded by a **sākin** yāʾ |
| لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ | **followed** by a sākin lām |

The absence of the small wāw/yāʾ in print is the reliable signal — trust the
mushaf before you trust the rule.

## Ḥafṣ exceptions

Two places break the general rule and must be memorised, not derived — both are
in [[Hafs-Special-Words]]:

- **يَرْضَهُ لَكُمْ** (Zumar 39:7) — conditions met, but **no ṣilah**.
- **فِيهِۦ مُهَانًا** (Furqān 25:69) — preceded by a sākin yāʾ, yet **ṣilah is
  given** (2 counts).

Two further places give the hāʾ a ḍammah with no ṣilah: أَنسَىٰنِيهُ (18:63) and
عَلَيْهُ ٱللَّهَ (48:10).

## Common mistakes

1. Giving ṣilah after a sākin letter — "min-hū" for مِنْهُ.
2. Keeping the ṣilah at waqf — it must drop: إِنَّهُۥ stops as `innah`.
3. Giving ṣilah kubrā only 2 counts, confusing it with ṣughrā.
4. Missing the small yāʾ, which is easy to overlook at small print sizes.

## cpfair coverage

**No cpfair key.** Ṣilah is not among the 18 keys in cpfair/quran-tajweed;
marking is hand-authored here, though the mushaf's own small wāw/yāʾ makes it
mechanically detectable from the Tanzīl text. See [[cpfair-quran-tajweed]].
