# Tarqīq ar-Rāʾ — تَرْقِيقُ الرَّاء

## Definition

The conditions under which [[Ra]] — the busiest letter in tajweed — is read
**light**. This is the half of the rā' rules that learners actually have to work
for: heavy is what most of them do by default, so every light rā' is a habit to be
unlearned.

The counterpart is [[Ra-Tafkhim]]; the words where both are transmitted are in
[[Ra-Jawaz-Wajhayn]].

## Trigger condition

### The baseline, in one line

> **kasra → light.**
> **A sākin rā' takes its ruling from the vowel BEFORE it — a kasra before it
> makes it light, provided that kasra is original and no heavy letter follows.**

### The full list

| # | Condition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ر carries a **kasra** | رِحْلَةَ (106:2) · رِزْقَهُۥ (89:16) |
| 2 | ر is **sākinah** after an **original kasra**, and **no ḥarf isti'lā' follows** in the same word | وَفِرْعَوْنَ (89:10) · ٱلْفِرْدَوْسِ (18:107) · وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ (110:3) · فَٱنتَصِرْ (54:10) |
| 3 | ر is **sākinah at waqf**, preceded by a **yā' sākinah** | خَيْرْ (97:3) |
| 4 | ر is **sākinah at waqf**, preceded by a sākin letter which is itself preceded by a **kasra** | ٱلسِّحْرْ (10:81) |
| 5 | ر is **at waqf** and carried a **kasra in waṣl** | مُقْتَدِرْ (54:42) |
| 6 | ر carries a **shaddah with a kasra** | شَرِّ (113:2) |

Condition 2 carries the two qualifiers that generate all the difficulty:

- **"original"** excludes the kasra of a hamzat waṣl, which disappears in waṣl.
  ٱرْجِعِىٓ is therefore **heavy** — see [[Ra-Tafkhim]] condition 5.
- **"no isti'lā' follows"** excludes the five reference words قِرْطَاسٍ · إِرْصَادًا
  · فِرْقَةٍ · مِرْصَادًا · لَبِٱلْمِرْصَادِ, which are heavy.

Note also that the isti'lā' letter must follow **in the same word**, and it is the
letter *after* the ر that matters — not one earlier in the word. وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ has
a غ in it, but the غ comes before the ر, so the ر stays light.

## How to perform it

1. Same makhraj as the heavy ر — tongue tip at the gum ridge. Only the **back** of
   the tongue changes: for tarqīq it stays **down**.
2. The vowel colour is forward and thin: *ri* as in English *rich*, not *ruh*.
3. **Still one tap.** Lightness does not mean a weaker contact; a slack tongue
   produces the trill (takrīr) just as reliably as an over-eager one.
4. Any alif or madd letter after a light ر is light: **رِحْلَةَ**, **كَرِيم**.

The first two are the highest-value drill words in the whole family: رِحْلَةَ sits
in Sūrat Quraysh and رِزْقًا / رِزْق recurs constantly, and both are read heavy by
almost every untrained reader.

## Common mistakes

- **Heavy by default.** رِزْقًا and فِرْعَوْن are the two words where this shows up
  most; if a learner gets those two right, the habit is broken.
- **Applying the waṣl ruling at waqf, or the reverse.** ٱلْقَدْرِ is light in waṣl
  and heavy at waqf; مُّقْتَدِرٍ is light in both, but for two different reasons.
  Always ask "am I stopping here?" before deciding.
- **Letting a heavy letter earlier in the word pull the ر heavy** —
  وَٱسْتَغْفِرْهُ read with a heavy ر because of the غ. Only a *following* isti'lā'
  letter counts.
- **Trilling**, which is as common on light rā's as heavy ones and is a separate
  fault from heaviness.
- **Thinning the ر so far it approaches a ل or a flap**, losing the letter's
  identity in the attempt to avoid heaviness.

## Contrast with

- **[[Ra-Tafkhim]]** — the mirror note; the two are one lesson and one quiz.
  The decisive distractors are: original kasra vs hamzat-waṣl kasra, and isti'lā'
  before the ر vs after it.
- **[[Ra-Jawaz-Wajhayn]]** — where the honest answer is "both".
- **[[Tarqiq]]** — the general default. ر is light *by rule* here; most letters are
  light by nature.
- **[[Lam-Jalalah]]** — the other switching letter, with one condition rather than
  six.

## Scholars differ

As with [[Ra-Tafkhim]], the substance is agreed for Ḥafṣ and the variation is in
grouping and count — some works give five tarqīq conditions by folding the shaddah
case into condition 1, some give six. Genuine transmitted difference is confined
to [[Ra-Jawaz-Wajhayn]].
