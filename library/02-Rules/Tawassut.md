# At-Tawassut — التوسط (al-Bayniyyah — البينية)

## Definition

**Linguistically**, *tawassut* is being in the middle; *bayniyyah* is "in-between-ness".

**Technically**, tawassut is the state of a letter whose sound is **neither completely
blocked nor completely flowing**. The airstream is obstructed at the makhraj but escapes
by another route — around the sides of the tongue (ل), through the nose (ن, م), by
deviation (ر), or through a narrowed but voiced throat (ع).

## Its opposite

Tawassut has no opposite of its own: it is the **midpoint of the [[Shiddah]] ↔
[[Rakhawah]] scale**. Classical works therefore count shiddah/rakhawah as one opposed
pair and treat tawassut as the intermediate degree, not as an eleventh sifah with a
partner. Some books call it a sifah with an opposite on both sides; the letters and
sounds described are identical either way.

## Letters

Five letters:

> **لِنْ عُمَرْ** — *lin ʿumar*

[[Lam|ل]] · [[Noon|ن]] · [[Ayn|ع]] · [[Meem|م]] · [[Ra|ر]]

## What it sounds like

The teacher checks whether the letter **can be held but does not hiss**. Ask for a
sustained sakin ل or ن: it continues, like rakhawah, but the continuation is resonant,
not fricative — the sound is coming out around or through, not past a narrow gap.

The two diagnostic failures are both on ع:

- **ع as a glottal stop** — the student produces ء, a hard catch in the throat. That is
  shiddah, not tawassut.
- **ع as an open vowel** — the student produces nothing but a strained "aa". That is
  rakhawah at best, and usually nothing at all.

Correct ع is squeezed at the middle of the throat and *audible while it is being held*.
This is among the most-cited errors for English, Turkish, Uzbek, Persian and Urdu speakers.

## Why it matters

- ن and م carry [[Ghunnah-sifah]]; their tawassut is precisely the fact that the sound
  escapes through the nose rather than being stopped or fricated.
- ل and ر carry [[Inhiraf]], and ر additionally carries [[Takrir]] — both are descriptions
  of *how* the sound gets out of a partially-blocked makhraj.
- Because tawassut letters are not shiddah, none of them takes qalqalah, however
  "bouncy" a sakin ر may feel to a beginner. See [[Qalqalah-sifah]].
