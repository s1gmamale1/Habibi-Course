# Waqf Signs — رموز الأوقاف

> ### ⚠ Status: needs-review — the sign set depends on which mushaf you hold
>
> **The waqf signs are not part of the revealed text.** They are a later
> scholarly aid, chiefly the work of Shaykh Muḥammad ibn ʿAlī Khalaf
> al-Ḥusaynī "al-Hamadhānī" (d. 1357 AH), and **different printing traditions
> use different sets**:
>
> | Tradition | Character of the set |
> |---|---|
> | **Madinah (King Fahd)** | the set taught below |
> | **Indo-Pak (Tāj / Persian)** | partly different — ط for waqf muṭlaq, ز for mujawwaz, plus the ع rukūʿ marker |
> | **Turkish / Ottoman** | a further variant |
>
> **Teach the set matching the mushaf the learner actually holds.** For Uzbek
> and wider Central Asian learners the **Indo-Pak set is very likely what is in
> their hands**, not the Madinah set. This note is marked `needs-review` until
> the mushaf edition targeted by the course is fixed and the sign table is
> checked against a physical copy of it.

## The six signs this course teaches (v1)

| Sign | Arabic name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **م** | الوقف اللازم | **Must stop.** Joining would corrupt the meaning. |
| **لا** | لا وقف فيه | **Do not stop.** If it falls at an āyah end you may stop, but must resume from before. |
| **ج** | الوقف الجائز | Stopping and joining are **equally permissible**. |
| **صلى** | الوصل أولى | Both permitted; **joining is better**. |
| **قلى** | الوقف أولى | Both permitted; **stopping is better**. |
| **س / سكتة** | السكتة | **Brief pause without breathing** — see [[Sakt]]. |

Six is enough to read fluently and safely. Learn these; treat any other mark as
"a stop is discussed here" and stop at the āyah end instead.

## Explicitly excluded from v1

- **∴ … ∴ muʿānaqah** (المعانقة) — three dots at **two** places: stop at one of
  them, **never both**. Excluded because it needs the tām/kāfī framework of
  [[Waqf-Types]] to be secure first.
- **ق (قيل عليه الوقف)** — "it has been said one may stop here", but joining is
  better. Excluded because learners reliably confuse it with **قلى**.

Also outside v1: ص (murakhkhaṣ), صل (qad yūṣal), ز (mujawwaz), ك (kadhālik),
وقفة (a longer saktah).

## How to read the signs honestly

1. **م and لا are instructions.** Follow them.
2. **ج, صلى, قلى are advice.** They record a scholar's judgement about meaning;
   they are not binding, and reciters do differ.
3. **The rosette ۝ outranks everything for a beginner.** Stopping at an āyah end
   is always permitted, so a learner who is short of breath should aim for the
   next rosette rather than guess.
4. **س is not a stop at all** — no breath is taken. See [[Sakt]].
