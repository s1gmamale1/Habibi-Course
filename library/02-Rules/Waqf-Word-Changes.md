# How Words Change When You Stop — تغير الكلمة عند الوقف

Stopping is not just silence. **The last word physically changes shape.** A
learner who stops without applying these changes produces a word that does not
exist in Arabic.

## The nine changes

| # | Change | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Final ḥarakah drops** | The last letter becomes sākin. | يَعْلَمُونَ → يَعْلَمُونْ · ٱلرَّحِيمِ → ٱلرَّحِيمْ |
| 2 | **Tāʾ marbūṭah → sākin hāʾ** | ة becomes هْ. | ٱلْجَنَّةِ → ٱلْجَنَّهْ · رَحْمَةٌ → رَحْمَهْ |
| 3 | **Tanwīn fatḥ → alif** | 2 counts — [[Madd-Iwad]]. | أَفْوَاجًا → أَفْوَاجَا |
| 4 | **Tanwīn ḍamm / kasr → dropped** | Simply becomes sukūn, no madd. | أَحَدٌ → أَحَدْ · نُورٍ → نُورْ |
| 5 | **Madd ʿāriḍ appears** | A preceding madd letter now takes 2/4/6. | نَسْتَعِينُ → نَسْتَعِينْ — [[Madd-Arid-Lissukun]] |
| 6 | **Madd līn appears** | A preceding līn letter now takes 2/4/6. | خَوْفٍ → خَوْفْ — [[Madd-Leen]] |
| 7 | **Qalqalah kubrā** | If the final letter is a qalqalah letter. | أَحَدٌ → أَحَدْ (bounced) |
| 8 | **Madd ṣilah drops** | The connective madd on the hāʾ vanishes. | إِنَّهُۥ → إِنَّهْ — [[Madd-Silah]] |
| 9 | **Shaddah retained** | A final shaddah is held with emphasis. | وَتَبَّ → وَتَبّْ · ٱلْحَقُّ → ٱلْحَقّْ |

### The tāʾ marbūṭah exception

Where the mushaf writes an **open tāʾ** — رَحْمَتَ, نِعْمَتَ, سُنَّتَ,
ٱمْرَأَتَ — it stops as a plain sākin **تْ**, not as a hāʾ. This is spelling,
not grammar: read what is written.

## Rawm and ishmām — the two refinements

At waqf you may optionally show the vowel that was dropped:

| Term | Arabic | What you do | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rawm** | الروم | Pronounce about **one third** of the final vowel — audible but very soft | **ḍammah and kasrah only**, never fatḥah |
| **Ishmām** | الإشمام | **Round the lips** immediately after the sukūn, silently — visible to a watcher, inaudible | **ḍammah only** |

Both are optional. Ishmām is also the mechanism behind لَا تَأْمَ۫نَّا (Yūsuf
12:11) — see [[Hafs-Special-Words]], where it is the only ishmām in Ḥafṣ.

## Why this belongs in Unit 2

Four separate madd rules ([[Madd-Iwad]], [[Madd-Arid-Lissukun]], [[Madd-Leen]],
and the disappearance of [[Madd-Silah]]) are *consequences* of this one table.
Teach the table first and those rules become observations rather than new
material.

## Common mistakes

1. Stopping with the ḥarakah still on — "al-ʿālamīna" with an audible fatḥah.
2. Stopping on a tāʾ marbūṭah as "-ah-t" or, worse, "-atā".
3. Forgetting the compensating alif on tanwīn fatḥ.
4. Keeping the ṣilah madd at a stop.
