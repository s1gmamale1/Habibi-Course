# ب — bāʾ

## Makhraj

**Point 16 of 17** — *ash-shafatān* (الشَّفَتَان), **both lips**, pressed together and fully closed. Zone 4. It shares this makhraj with [[Meem]] and [[Waw]], and the difference is entirely in what happens behind the closure:

| Letter | Lips | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **ب** | closed **firmly** | air is blocked, pressure builds, released with a pop (shiddah + qalqalah) |
| **م** | closed **lightly** | air escapes through the nose (ghunnah) |
| **و** | rounded, **not closed** | air flows through the gap |

Teacher's script: *"Close your lips completely, hold the air for a moment — you should feel your cheeks want to puff — then let it go. No nose, no leaking."*

## Sifat

| Sifah | Opposite | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Jahr** (voiced) | hams | Voiced. Fingers on the throat: buzzing already during the closure. |
| **Shiddah** (plosive) | rakhāwah | Complete block then release. One of the eight أَجِدْ قَطٍ بَكَتْ letters. It cannot be held as a sound. |
| **Istifāl** (lowered) | istiʿlāʾ | **Light, always.** Even in بِٱلصَّبْرِ, sandwiched against a heavy ص, the ب stays light. |
| **Infitāḥ** (open) | iṭbāq | No clamping. |
| **Idhlāq** (fluency) | iṣmāt | One of the six فِرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ letters — quick and easy. |
| **Qalqalah** (echo) | — | One of the five قُطْبُ جَدٍّ letters. Because ب is shiddah + jahr, a sākin ب cannot be voiced without an audible release. |

## Common mistakes

- **Omitting qalqalah on a sākin ب** (taxonomy §2.16) — بِٱلصَّبْرِ read with the lips simply stuck together. Correction cue: *"You stopped and left it there. Open the lips and let a small bounce out — `ṣab-(e)`. Not a full vowel, just an echo."* The echo has no vowel colour: it is neither `ba` nor `bi` nor `bu`.
- **Over-doing qalqalah** — adding a clear fathah, `ṣaba-r`. Correction cue: *"Half the size. It's a knock on a door, not a word."*
- **Aspirating it** — Urdu and Hindi speakers distinguish ب from بھ; some carry a light puff into the Arabic ب. Correction cue: *"No breath after the lips open. Voice only."*
- **Devoicing at word-end** — Turkish, Uzbek and Russian all devoice final stops, so a final ب drifts toward `p`. Correction cue: *"Fingers on the throat — the buzz must last until the lips open."*
- **Confusing with پ** — Persian, Urdu and Uzbek learners have a `p` phoneme that Arabic does not. Loanwords may pull ب toward `p`.
- **Letting the heavy neighbour bleed in** — بِٱلصَّبْرِ, ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ. The ب must stay light next to ص, ض, ط, ظ, غ, خ, ق.
- **Incomplete lip closure**, producing something between ب and [[Waw]].

## Confused with

- **پ (`p`)** — not an Arabic letter; the live substitute for Persian, Urdu and Uzbek L1 speakers, and the shape a devoiced final ب takes.
- **[[Meem]] (م)** — same makhraj, but م lets air through the nose. Pinch the nose: on ب nothing changes; on م the sound is blocked.
- **[[Waw]] (و)** — same zone; و does not close the lips.
- **[[Fa]] (ف)** — if the lips part too early and the air hisses out, ب slides toward ف.

## In tajweed

- **Qalqalah** — ṣughrā when the ب is sākin mid-word (بِٱلصَّبْرِ); kubrā when the sukūn arises from stopping on it at a waqf.
- **Iqlāb** — a nūn sākinah or tanwīn followed by ب is converted into a **mīm** with a two-count ghunnah and the lips left slightly apart: أَنۢبَآءِ (11:49), مِنۢ بَعْدِ. The muṣḥaf marks it with a small م above the nūn. This is ب's signature rule.
- **Ikhfāʾ shafawī** — a **mīm sākinah** followed by ب is hidden with a two-count ghunnah, lips lightly together but not pressed: تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ.
- **Idhlāq** — one of the six فِرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ letters.
- **Tarqīq** — always light; ب is never heavy.
