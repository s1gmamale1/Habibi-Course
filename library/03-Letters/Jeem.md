# ج — jīm

## Makhraj

The **3rd of the ten tongue makharij**: **wasat al-lisan** — the *middle* of the tongue pressed against the palate directly above it. Not the tip, not the back. The whole broad middle of the tongue makes contact and blocks the sound completely, then releases.

ج shares this makhraj with [[Sheen]] and [[Ya]]. Together these three are called **الْحُرُوفُ الشَّجْرِيَّة**. They come from the same place and are told apart only by sifat: ج stops the sound completely (shiddah), ش lets it flow and spread (rakhawah + tafashshi), ي glides.

Cue: *"Flatten the middle of your tongue up against the roof of your mouth — a wide contact, not a point — and then break it."*

## Sifat

| Sifah | Opposite | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **jahr** (voiced) | hams | The cords buzz. There is no breath escaping on release. |
| **shiddah** (plosive) | rakhawah | **The sound is fully blocked and then burst.** This is what makes ج a "d͡ʒ", not a "ʒ". |
| **istifal** (lowered) | isti'la | Light. ج is never heavy — not even beside ط or ص. |
| **infitah** (open) | itbaq | No clamping. |
| **ismat** | idhlaq | Not a fluent letter. |
| **qalqalah** | — | shiddah + jahr means a sakin ج must bounce out. |

## Common mistakes

- **Producing a French "j"** — the soft *zh* of *bonjour*, or the Persian/Urdu ژ. This is the big one for Persian and Urdu speakers, whose languages have both ج and ژ and who blur them in recitation. That sound has no shiddah: it flows. It is a different letter. Say يَجْعَلْ with the fricative and let the student hear that the ج has gone soft and mushy.
- **Producing a hard "g"** (Egyptian colloquial ج). The makhraj slides all the way back to ق/ك territory.
- **Dropping the qalqalah** on sakin ج: يَجْعَلْ becomes "yaj'al" with the ج merely leaned on instead of released.
- **Turkish and Uzbek speakers** usually have a native "c/j" (Turkish *cam*, Uzbek *jon*) that is close and usable — but it is often produced further forward, with the tongue tip. Push the contact back to the middle of the tongue.

**Spoken correction cue:** *"Stop the sound completely first — hold it — then let it go. If you can sing it, it's wrong."*

## Confused with

- [[Sheen]] — same makhraj, opposite flow. ج blocks, ش spreads.
- [[Ya]] — same makhraj. A weak ج collapses toward ي; an over-hardened ي becomes ج.
- [[Zay]] and [[Dhal]] — when ج is softened into "zh", it lands next to the z-family.

## In tajweed

- **[[Qalqalah-sifah|Qalqalah]].** ج is the ج of **قُطْبُ جَدٍّ**. Sakin ج bounces: [[Qalqalah-Sughra|qalqalah sughra]] mid-word (يَجْعَلْ), [[Qalqalah-Kubra|qalqalah kubra]] at a stop.
- **[[Ikhfa-Haqiqi|Ikhfa']].** ج is one of the fifteen ikhfa' letters — noon sakinah before ج is hidden with a light ghunnah, as in أَنْ جَاءَهُ.
- **[[Istifal|Istifal]] / [[Tarqiq|tarqeeq]]** — always light, and it does not make a following ر heavy.
- ج is not an idgham letter for noon sakinah.
