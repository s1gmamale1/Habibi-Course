# م — mīm

## Makhraj

**Point 16 of 17** — *ash-shafatān* (الشَّفَتَان), **both lips**, closed **lightly**. Zone 4. Shared with [[Ba]] and [[Waw]].

But م has a second address: its accompanying **ghunnah** issues from **point 17, al-khayshūm (الْخَيْشُوم)**, the nasal cavity. The lips shape the letter; the nose carries the resonance. Every م in the Qurʾān has some ghunnah — the question is only how long.

Teacher's script: *"Close your lips softly — softer than for ب — and hum. The sound is going out of your nose, not your mouth."*

**The nose-pinch test (use it constantly):** *"Pinch your nose and say مَ. The sound must be strangled and blocked. If you can say it comfortably with your nose pinched, there is no ghunnah — you are making a ب with the lips open."*

## Sifat

| Sifah | Opposite | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Jahr** (voiced) | hams | Fully voiced. |
| **Tawassuṭ** (between) | shiddah / rakhāwah | Neither blocked nor freely flowing — one of the five لِنْ عُمَرْ letters. The mouth is closed, but the sound continues through the nose. |
| **Istifāl** (lowered) | istiʿlāʾ | Light, always — even beside heavy letters. |
| **Infitāḥ** (open) | iṭbāq | No clamping. |
| **Idhlāq** (fluency) | iṣmāt | One of the six فِرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ letters. |
| **Ghunnah** (nasalisation) | — | Inseparable from م and ن; issues from al-khayshūm. Lengthened to two counts in specific states only. |

### The five levels of ghunnah, strongest to weakest

Teach these as a ladder; most errors are a level applied in the wrong place.

1. **Mushaddadah** — ثُمَّ, إِنَّ — full two counts.
2. **Mudghamah** — a mīm sākinah merging into a following mīm (idghām shafawī) — full two counts.
3. **Mukhfāh** — mīm sākinah before [[Ba]] (ikhfāʾ shafawī) — two counts.
4. **Sākinah with izhār** — أَنْعَمْتَ, لَمْ يَلِدْ — short; **no stretch**.
5. **Mutaḥarrikah** — مَٰلِكِ — shortest of all; just the natural nasal colour of the letter.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying a full two-count ghunnah to levels 4 and 5** (taxonomy §3.19) — the commonest ghunnah error by far, and it comes from students who have just learned ghunnah and are proud of it. أَنْعَمْتَ read `anʿammmta`. Correction cue: *"Close, touch, open. Don't sit on it. Save the long hum for the shaddah."*
- **Producing the ghunnah in the mouth as a hum instead of through the nose** (taxonomy §2.17). Correction cue: *"Pinch your nose. If the sound survives, it isn't coming from the right place."*
- **Failing to close the lips fully**, which weakens or kills the ghunnah (taxonomy §2.16). Correction cue: *"Lips together — all the way, but gently. I should not see any gap."*
- **Leaving the lips glued together after a sākin mīm** so the next letter is swallowed: أَنْعَمْتَ where the ت never gets a clean start.
- **Pressing the lips hard**, turning م into a ب-like closure and cutting the nasal flow.
- **Turkic / Persian / Urdu L1** — م itself is easy in all of these; the errors above are about *rule application*, not about the sound. Praise the letter, drill the rules.
- **Nasalising letters that carry no ghunnah** — over-generalisation after the ghunnah lessons. Only م and ن have it.

## Confused with

- **[[Ba]] (ب)** — same makhraj, closed lips. ب blocks the air completely and pops; م lets it out through the nose. Nose-pinch test separates them instantly.
- **[[Noon]] (ن)** — the other ghunnah letter, and the reason iqlāb exists. م is made at the lips; ن at the tongue tip against the upper gums. Students who are hearing "the humming letter" rather than the letter itself will blur them.
- **[[Waw]] (و)** — same zone, lips open. A weak, unclosed م drifts here.

## In tajweed

م sits at the centre of more rules than any other letter in Phase 1:

- **Ahkām al-mīm as-sākinah** — three rules:
  - **Ikhfāʾ shafawī** — م sākinah + [[Ba]]: hidden, two-count ghunnah, lips lightly together.
  - **Idghām shafawī (mithlayn ṣaghīr)** — م sākinah + م: merged, two-count ghunnah.
  - **Izhār shafawī** — م sākinah + any of the other 26 letters: clear, **no stretch**. Watch [[Fa]] and [[Waw]] especially, where the makhārij are close.
- **Iqlāb** — a nūn sākinah or tanwīn before [[Ba]] is converted **into a mīm** with a two-count ghunnah. The م that appears here is not written as a letter; it is marked with a small م above the nūn.
- **Idghām with ghunnah** — م is one of the يَرْمَلُونَ letters: a nūn sākinah or tanwīn before م merges into it with ghunnah (مِن مَّاءٍ).
- **Mīm mushaddadah** — always a full two-count ghunnah (ثُمَّ, أُمَّة).
- **Al-khayshūm** — the makhraj of the ghunnah itself; teach it as the 17th point when teaching م and ن.
- **No qalqalah, no tafkhīm.**
