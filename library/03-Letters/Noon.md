# ن — nūn

## Makhraj

**ن is the only letter in the alphabet with two makharij at once, and you must teach both.**

1. **The oral component** — the **6th of the ten tongue makharij**: the **tip of the tongue** against the **gums of the two upper incisors**, a little **below** the contact point of [[Lam]]. This is where the body of the letter is formed.
2. **The nasal component** — **al-khayshum**, the nasal passage. This is the makhraj of **ghunnah**, and it is not optional: ghunnah accompanies ن in *every* state it ever occurs in. It is part of the letter, not a rule applied to it.

Cue: *"Tip of the tongue on the gum, just under where you put it for ل — and the sound must come out of your nose at the same time."*

**The pinch test.** Have the student hold a ن and pinch their nose shut. The sound must be **blocked**. If it keeps coming out of the mouth unchanged, there is no ghunnah and the letter is not being made.

## Sifat

| Sifah | Opposite | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **jahr** (voiced) | hams | Voiced throughout. |
| **tawassut** (in between) | shiddah / rakhawah | Neither stopped nor freely flowing — one of **لِنْ عُمَرْ**. |
| **istifal** (lowered) | isti'la | Light by default. |
| **infitah** (open) | itbaq | No clamping. |
| **idhlaq** (fluency) | ismat | One of the six easy letters, **فِرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ**. |
| **ghunnah** (nasalisation) | — | Shared only with م. Issues from al-khayshum. |

**The five levels of ghunnah**, strongest to weakest — this is the scale a student is graded on:

1. **Mushaddad** — إِنَّ, ٱلنَّاسِ — full 2 counts.
2. **Mudgham** — مِن مَّاءٍ — full 2 counts.
3. **Mukhfa** — مِن قَبْلُ — full 2 counts.
4. **Sakin with izhar** — مِنْ هَادٍ — short.
5. **Mutaharrik** (with a vowel) — نَعْبُدُ — shortest.

## Common mistakes

- **Nasalising every ن for two counts.** The most common over-correction. ghunnah is *always present* but only *lengthened* at levels 1–3. The ن of نَعْبُدُ gets the shortest possible ghunnah, not a held hum.
- **No nasal component at all** — the student makes a clean oral "n" like an English *n* in a hurry. Use the pinch test.
- **Producing the ghunnah in the mouth as a hum** rather than through the nose. Sounds nasal, isn't.
- **Sliding the tongue back up to the ل position**, blurring ن toward ل.
- **Uzbek, Turkish and Persian speakers** have a native "n" that is usually placed correctly, but their ghunnah is weak or absent because their languages carry no such systematic nasal resonance; and Turkish/Uzbek speakers tend to assimilate a final ن to a following consonant automatically, which pre-empts the actual ikhfa'/idgham rules.
- **Reading tanween as a written ن** — the tanween *is* a noon sakinah in sound but is dropped at a stop.

**Spoken correction cue:** *"Tip on the gum, and hum it out of your nose. Pinch your nose — if the sound survives, you're not doing it."*

## Confused with

- [[Lam]] — the makhraj directly above it.
- [[Meem]] — the other ghunnah letter, from the lips. ن and م are taught together because every ghunnah rule applies to both.
- Tanween — not a letter, but sounds identical to a sakin ن and must obey the same four rules.

## In tajweed

ن is the busiest letter in the rulebook after [[Ra]]. Noon sakinah and tanween carry **four rules**:

- **[[Izhar-Halqi|Izhar halqi]]** — before the six throat letters (ء ه ع ح غ خ): clear, short ghunnah, no merging. مِنْ هَادٍ.
- **Idgham** — before the six letters of **يَرْمَلُونَ**: [[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah|with ghunnah]] for ي ن م و (incomplete for ي and و — the letter is still heard), [[Idgham-Bila-Ghunnah|without ghunnah]] for ل and ر. مَن يَعْمَلْ, مِن لَّدُنْهُ.
- **[[Iqlab|Iqlab]]** — before ب: the ن turns into a hidden م with 2 counts of ghunnah. مِنۢ بَعْدِ.
- **[[Ikhfa-Haqiqi|Ikhfa']]** — before the remaining fifteen letters: the ن is hidden, tongue does not touch the gum, 2 counts of ghunnah, made heavy or light according to the following letter. مِن قَبْلُ (heavy), مِن شَرِّ (light).

Plus:

- **Noon mushaddadah** (نّ) — always 2 counts of [[Ghunnah|ghunnah]], wherever it appears: إِنَّ, ٱلنَّاسِ.
- **Izhar mutlaq** — the four words دُنْيَا, بُنْيَان, صِنْوَان, قِنْوَان, where ن sakinah meets ي or و *inside one word* and is read clear instead of merged.
- ن is not a [[Qalqalah-sifah|qalqalah]] letter.
