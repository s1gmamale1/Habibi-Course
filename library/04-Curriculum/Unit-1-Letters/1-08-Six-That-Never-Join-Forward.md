## Objectives

- Student can name the 6 non-connecting letters (ا د ذ ر ز و) and state the one rule they share: they never join to the letter that follows them, even mid-word.
- Student can read a cluster containing a non-connector and correctly identify the resulting visual "break" rather than misreading it as a joined shape.
- Student can distinguish, on sight, whether a gap between two letters in a cluster is (a) a genuine word-break or (b) simply a non-connector letter doing what it always does.

## Hook

Give her **أَحَدٌ** and ask her to point at where one letter ends and the next begins.

She will hesitate at the gap after the د — because there is one, and last lesson told her letters join. **That gap is not a space between words and it is not a mistake.** Six letters in the alphabet refuse to connect to whatever follows them, and every one of them leaves a visible break in the middle of a word.

This is the lesson that stops her reading one word as two, which is the single most common cold-reading error at this stage.

## What they already know

From 1-07: that letters join, and that the sound survives the shape change. Today is the exception to that rule — and it is a closed list of six, not a pattern she has to derive.

## Teaching sequence

### 1. 6 letters that never connect forward

- ا د ذ ر ز و never connect to the letter AFTER them — they still connect fine to the letter BEFORE them.
- This is a fixed, closed list — no other letters behave this way.
- All 6 were already learned in Unit 1.1 — today is pure mechanics, no new sounds.

### 2. Worked example: د in a cluster

- د never connects forward, so whatever letter follows it keeps its own isolated/initial-looking shape rather than a joined medial shape.
- Isolated / word-initial shape (no forward join): د
- Final shape (preceded by a joiner): ـد

### 3. Worked example: ر in a cluster

- ر behaves the same way — no forward join, ever.
- Isolated / word-initial shape: ر
- Final shape (preceded by a joiner): ـر

### 4. Worked example: و in a cluster

- و behaves the same way — no forward join, ever.
- Isolated / word-initial shape: و
- Final shape (preceded by a joiner): ـو

**In plain terms:** Six letters — ا د ذ ر ز و — connect to the letter before them but never to the letter after. The gap they leave is inside the word, not between words.

## Drills

**Contrast drill — non-connector gap vs. genuine word-break.** Same letters, only the spacing differs: one is a single connected cluster where a non-connector just did its normal job; the other is a real two-word boundary. Compare the reading rhythm.

**Guided recognition drill — predict the break.** Look at each unvoweled cluster below with a non-connector circled. Before reading aloud, predict where the joined/unjoined shape boundary will fall, then read it aloud to confirm.

**Reading drill — mixed connectors and non-connectors.** Read each cluster aloud; each contains at least one non-connector, positioned in different spots (first, middle, last) so the rule is tested regardless of position within the cluster.

**Cumulative check — re-read lesson 1.7's clusters.** Re-read 2 of lesson 1.7's clusters, now correctly explaining why they joined the way they did (neither contains any of today's 6 non-connectors, so both joined normally).

**Concept flashcard — the 6 non-connectors.** Chant the list aloud as a group, round after round, until it feels automatic.

**Cluster-reading drill (unvoweled).** Read each cluster aloud. Each one contains at least one non-connector, positioned in different spots — first, middle, last — so the rule applies no matter where it falls.

**"Predict the break" drill.** Before reading aloud, predict where the joined/unjoined shape boundary will fall in each cluster below, then read it aloud to confirm.

## Listen-for

The teacher's checklist as it ships. **These are the shipped plain-string form**,
not the structured `ListenForSchema` the tajweed units use — see the banner above.

- Student trying to force a non-connector into a joined medial shape that doesn't exist for it — a very natural over-generalization error at this stage, gently correct by re-showing the fixed list.
- Student misreading a non-connector's normal "gap" as a full word-break, splitting one word into two when reading aloud.
- Regression on the 6 individual letters' own sounds (since these are "old" content from Unit 1.1, a quick sound-accuracy spot-check is worthwhile alongside the shape-mechanics focus).

## Teacher script

- Open with a quick review of lesson 1.7's cluster-reading before introducing today's mechanics.
- Present the 6 non-connectors (ا د ذ ر ز و) as one fixed, closed list — drill it as a group chant until automatic before anything else.
- Model د, ر, and و each in a cluster, showing explicitly that the letter following each one keeps its own isolated/initial shape rather than a joined medial shape.
- Contrast a non-connector's normal internal gap against a genuine two-word space side by side, so the student learns the visual difference.
- Run the 'predict the break' drill before reading aloud — the student should predict, then confirm, not read cold.
- Re-read 2 of lesson 1.7's clusters at the end, now explaining why they joined the way they did.

## Homework

Daily recitation (aloud) of the 6 non-connector clusters from today's drill, 3× through, reviewing letters and clusters learned in previous lessons. Student attempts the "predict the break" exercise on 3 new clusters the teacher provides for next lesson (recognition/prediction only, no writing). Recite the fixed 6-letter list (ا د ذ ر ز و) from memory once daily. No printed sheets.
