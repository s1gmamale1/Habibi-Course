## Objectives

- Student can read any 2–3 letter unvoweled cluster built from any combination of the 28 letters, correctly identifying each component letter regardless of its positional shape.
- Student demonstrates the non-connector rule applying correctly and automatically, without needing the rule restated.
- Student can read at least 30 real unvoweled Arabic words containing non-connectors, predicting each mid-word break before reading it.
- Student's cluster-reading pace has visibly increased from lesson 1.7's baseline (informal, non-graded pacing check only).

## Hook

No new material today, so open by testing rather than reviewing.

Put four unseen joined clusters in front of her and say nothing but *read them*. Whatever breaks is the curriculum for the session. **Do not pre-empt it by revising first** — a revision lesson that warms her up before testing measures the warm-up, not the skill.

## What they already know

Everything in Unit 1.2: the four positional forms, and the six letters that break the chain. She has all the pieces; what she has not had is a session where nobody tells her which rule is coming.

## Teaching sequence

### 1. Everything you need to read any cluster

- Rule 1 (lesson 1.7): a letter's sound never changes, but its SHAPE changes by position — isolated, initial, medial, final.
- Rule 2 (lesson 1.8): 6 letters — ا د ذ ر ز و — never connect forward, no matter where they sit.
- That's it. Those two rules are everything needed to read any connected cluster of the 28 letters.

### 2. Error-log review

- Teacher and student look together at which letters or cluster types caused hesitation this lesson.
- Log by category: positional-shape confusion, non-connector confusion, or sound-only confusion — not just a pass/fail tally.
- This becomes direct Checkpoint 1 prep data.

### 3. Coming next: vowels

- Next unit — we add vowels, and these same letters start to really come alive.

**In plain terms:** Nothing new today. If you can look at a joined word and see where each letter starts and stops, Unit 1.2 has done its job.

## Drills

**Guided reading set 1 — cross-family 2-letter clusters.** These deliberately mix distant shape-families — an early letter beside a late one — so no cluster can be guessed from alphabet order. Read right to left, name both letters and the form each took.

**Guided reading set 2 — cross-family 3-letter clusters.** Three letters, three different families each time. These are not words — they are decoding practice, built so that every letter sits in a position you have to identify rather than recognize.

**Guided reading set 3 — non-connectors mixed throughout.** Every cluster here contains at least one of the 6 non-connectors (ا د ذ ر ز و) from lesson 1.8. Before reading, predict where the joining will break — then read and confirm.

**Reading set A — 15 real words with non-connectors.** Real, unvoweled Arabic words. Unlike lesson 1.7's sets, these contain non-connectors, so the joining breaks mid-word — exactly the case rule 2 covers. For each word: spot the non-connector, predict the break, then read right to left.

**Reading set B — 15 more real words with non-connectors.** Same task. Some of these break twice. Read right to left, naming each letter and its form before blending.

**Independent reading round — cold read.** Eight words the student has not seen in any previous lesson, drill or homework sheet. All connectors, no breaks. Teacher does not prompt unless the student is genuinely stuck — this is the transfer check that predicts Checkpoint 1 performance.

**Pacing check — timed 12-word set.** Informal timing only, compared lightly against the student's lesson 1.7 pace. Encouragement framing, never a graded metric. Mixed connectors and non-connectors, all previously read.

## Listen-for

The teacher's checklist as it ships. **These are the shipped plain-string form**,
not the structured `ListenForSchema` the tajweed units use — see the banner above.

- Any letter from the high-risk six (ح, ض, ع, ق, the ذ/ز pair, ص) resurfacing as an error once embedded in a real word rather than read in isolation — the most diagnostic moment before Checkpoint 1.
- Hesitation specifically at non-connector transitions — confirms whether lesson 1.8's rule is automatic or still consciously recalled.
- Medial ـهـ and ـعـ misread inside real words even though the flashcard forms were correct in 1.7.
- Student splitting a single word into two at a non-connector gap (reading بخار or تلميذ as two words).
- Increasing hesitation as words grow from 3 to 4–5 letters — indicates letter-by-letter sounding rather than chunking. Normal here; note it for Phase 2 planning.
- Reading pace on the cold-read set versus the rehearsed sets — a large gap means recognition is memorised, not decoded.

## Teacher script

- This is the Unit 1.2 consolidation lesson and the last full lesson before vowels. Nothing new is taught — everything today is transfer.
- Restate the two rules once (slide 3) and then never restate them again. If the student needs the rule mid-drill, log it: that is a checkpoint risk.
- Guided sets 1 and 2 deliberately mix distant shape-families so nothing can be guessed from alphabet order. Sets from lesson 1.7 were within-family; these are not.
- Guided set 3 is prediction-first: student says where the joining will break before reading, then reads to confirm. Do not let them read first and rationalize after.
- Reading sets A and B are the heart of the lesson — 30 real words, all containing non-connectors. This is the first time the student reads at real-word scale.
- Run the cold read strictly: 8 genuinely unseen words, no prompting unless truly stuck. This is the single best predictor of Checkpoint 1 performance — log every hesitation.
- Error-log review is done together, out loud, categorised (positional-shape / non-connector / sound-only). This feeds the Checkpoint 1 revision map directly.
- Pacing check is encouragement framing only. If the pace has not moved since 1.7, that is data for you, not a message for the student.
- Close on the vowels preview — the student should leave wanting lesson 1.10.

## Homework

Reading sets A and B (30 words with non-connectors) aloud 2× daily with break-prediction, the 8 cold-read words until instant, one self-timed run of the 12-word pacing set, and positional forms of 2–3 tricky letters written 4 rows each.

**Daily checklist:**

- Read the 30 words in Reading sets A and B aloud, 2× through, predicting each break before reading.
- Read the 8 cold-read words until they come without hesitation.
- Self-time the 12-word pacing set once, and note which words were slowest.
- Write the positional forms of 2–3 letters that gave you trouble, 4 rows each, saying each name and sound aloud as you write.

**Recap — Full 28-letter shuffle — cumulative confidence check:** ا · ب · ت · ث · ج · ح · خ · د · ذ · ر · ز · س · ش · ص · ض · ط · ظ · ع · غ · ف · ق · ك · ل · م · ن · ه · و · ي
