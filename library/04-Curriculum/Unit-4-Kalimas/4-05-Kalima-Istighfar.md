# Lesson 4.5 — Kalima Istighfār

> **`needs-review`.** Not Qurʾān; not corpus-verifiable; Arabic kept out of `examples:` —
> see [[4-01-Kalima-Tayyiba]]. Check the wording against your student's printed primer,
> especially the closing formula (some primers end at *ʿallāmu-l-ghuyūb* and omit *wa
> sattāru-l-ʿuyūb wa ghaffāru-dh-dhunūb*), and log it in [[Verification-Log]].

## Objectives

- Student can recite Kalima Istighfār from memory with correct tajweed on a planned breath
  scheme.
- Student can produce a **light** [[Lam-Jalalah]] in *bi-llāh* and say why it is light.
- Student can produce [[Iqlab]] at *dhambin* and *adhnabtuhū* — the first iqlābs in the
  unit.
- Student can name all three nūn-sākinah treatments occurring in this one kalima: iẓhār,
  ikhfāʾ and iqlāb.

## Hook

Four lessons in, she has said the name of Allah heavy every single time. Today it flips,
and the flip is the lesson.

Write these two, one above the other, and ask her to read both:

> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ
> إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

She will read them the same. They are not the same.

> **"Same word. Same letters. One is heavy and one is light, and the only thing that
> decides it is the vowel you said just before it."**

That is the moment the [[Lam-Jalalah]] rule stops being a fact she can recite and becomes
a decision she has to make. It has been waiting since 4-01 for a kalima that contains
both, and this is the one.

## The text

**أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ أَذْنَبْتُهُ عَمْدًا أَوْ خَطَأً سِرًّا أَوْ عَلَانِيَةً وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنَ الذَّنْبِ الَّذِي أَعْلَمُ وَمِنَ الذَّنْبِ الَّذِي لَا أَعْلَمُ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ وَسَتَّارُ الْعُيُوبِ وَغَفَّارُ الذُّنُوبِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ**

*astaghfiru-llāha rabbī min kulli dhambin adhnabtuhū ʿamdan aw khaṭaʾan sirran aw
ʿalāniyataw wa atūbu ilayhi mina-dh-dhambi-lladhī aʿlamu wa mina-dh-dhambi-lladhī lā
aʿlam, innaka anta ʿallāmu-l-ghuyūb wa sattāru-l-ʿuyūb wa ghaffāru-dh-dhunūb wa lā ḥawla
wa lā quwwata illā bi-llāhi-l-ʿaliyyi-l-ʿaẓīm*

> "I seek the forgiveness of Allah my Lord for every sin I have committed deliberately or
> by mistake, secretly or openly, and I repent to Him from the sin that I know and from
> the sin that I do not know. Indeed You are the Knower of hidden things, the Concealer of
> faults, the Forgiver of sins, and there is no power and no strength except with Allah,
> the Most High, the Most Great."

**Istighfār** (اِسْتِغْفَار) means *seeking forgiveness*.

## Rules occurring in it

### 1. أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ — [[Tafkhim]], [[Ra-Tafkhim]], heavy [[Lam-Jalalah]]

**غْ** — sākin ghayn, an elevated letter → **heavy**, held without a vowel to help. She met
this exact word in 4-04 §6.

**رُ** with ḍamma → **heavy**.

**اللَّهَ** — the preceding vowel is the **ḍamma** of *-ru* → **heavy**. Full, thick lām.

### 2. رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ — [[Ra-Tafkhim]] and [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]]

**رَ** with fatḥa → heavy. **بِّ** doubled, held.

**مِنْ كُلِّ** — nūn sākinah followed by **ك**. Kāf is one of the fifteen ikhfāʾ letters →
the nūn is **hidden**: not spoken cleanly, not merged, but sounded in the nose for **2
counts** while the tongue is already on its way to the kāf. *min kulli*, with a hum in the
middle.

Set it immediately against *min an* and *ʿanhu* from 4-04, where the nūn was fully
pronounced. **Same nūn sākinah, three words apart in her practice, opposite treatment**,
decided by the following letter. Throat letter → clear. One of the fifteen → hidden.

### 3. ذَنْبٍ — [[Iqlab]]

**The new rule of the lesson, and one of the most satisfying in tajweed.**

**نْ** followed by **ب**. Nūn sākinah or tanwīn before a bāʾ → **iqlāb**: the nūn is
*converted into a mīm*, hidden, with **2 counts of ghunnah**.

*dhanbin* is read **dhambin**. The lips close. There is no n in it at all.

The muṣḥaf prints a small **م** above the nūn to mark this; a primer may or may not. Tell
her the mark exists — she will meet it constantly in Unit 3's Qurʾānic work and it is
worth recognising here.

**أَذْنَبْتُهُ** — and immediately again: **نْ** … wait, look carefully. Here the nūn
carries a **fatḥa**, not a sukūn, so no iqlāb fires on it. What *does* fire is **بْ**
sākinah mid-word → [[Qalqalah-Sughra]], a clean bounce: *adh-nab-tuhū*. Also **ذْ**
sākinah, which is **not** a qalqalah letter and gets no bounce.

This pair — one word that looks like it should take iqlāb and does not, immediately after
one that does — is the best diagnostic in the kalima. She must **look at the vowel on the
nūn**, not at the presence of a bāʾ.

### 4. ذَنْبٍ أَذْنَبْتُهُ — [[Izhar-Halqi]]

The tanwīn kasr at the end of *dhambin* is followed by a **hamzah** → iẓhār, spoken
clearly.

So the same word contains **iqlāb inside it and iẓhār at its edge**, in that order. Say it
slowly: *dham … bin adhnabtuhū.* Lips close for the iqlāb, then the n at the end is fully
audible.

### 5. عَمْدًا أَوْ خَطَأً سِرًّا أَوْ — six rules in five words

[[Izhar-Shafawi]], [[Izhar-Halqi]] twice, [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]], [[Tafkhim]] and
[[Ra-Tafkhim]] — the densest stretch in the kalima. Take it word by word.

**عَمْدًا** — **مْ** sākinah followed by **د** → [[Izhar-Shafawi]], lips fully closed, mīm
clear, no hum onto the dāl.

**عَمْدًا أَوْ** — tanwīn fatḥ before a **hamzah** → [[Izhar-Halqi]], n spoken.

**خَطَأً** — two elevated letters back to back: **خ** and **ط**, both **heavy**
([[Tafkhim]]). Ṭāʾ additionally has iṭbāq — the tongue seals against the roof of the mouth
([[Itbaq]]) — and is the heaviest letter in this kalima. *khaṭaʾan*, fat and dark.

**خَطَأً سِرًّا** — tanwīn fatḥ before **س**, one of the fifteen → [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]],
hidden, 2 counts of hum.

**سِرًّا** — **رّ** doubled with a fatḥa → **heavy**, held for the length of two rāʾs.
A doubled heavy rāʾ is where over-rolling creeps in; heavy is a *thickness*, not a *trill*.

**سِرًّا أَوْ** — tanwīn before a hamzah → [[Izhar-Halqi]] again.

**أَوْ** — wāw sākinah after a fatḥa: a **līn** letter, flat and short while joining
([[Leen]]).

### 6. عَلَانِيَةً وَأَتُوبُ — [[Idgham-Maal-Ghunnah]]

Tanwīn fatḥ followed by **و** → idghām **with** ghunnah, doubled wāw, 2 counts of nose:
*ʿalāniyataw wa atūbu*. She built this in 4-04 §4 at *shayʾaw wa anā*; this is the same
join, and it should need no explanation now.

**إِلَيْهِ** — another **līn** (yāʾ sākinah after fatḥa), short while joining.

### 7. مِنَ الذَّنْبِ الَّذِي — [[Lam-Shamsiyyah]] ×2 and [[Iqlab]] again

**الذَّنْبِ** — **ذ** is a **sun** letter → the lām is silent and merged: *mina-dh-dhanbi*.
Look for the shadda on the dhāl; that is the whole read.

And inside it, **نْ** before **ب** → **iqlāb** again: *mina-dh-dhambi*.

**الَّذِي** — the lām of the article before another **ل**, itself a sun letter → merged:
*-lladhī*. This is the sun lām that students least often recognise, because the letter it
merges into is the same letter it is.

The whole phrase occurs **twice**, once with *aʿlamu* and once with *lā aʿlam*. Both times
identically. That repetition is a gift — use it to check consistency, which is the thing
that actually breaks in a long sentence.

### 8. إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ — [[Ghunnah]] and [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]]

**إِنَّكَ** — nūn mushaddadah → **2 counts of [[Ghunnah]]**.

**أَنْتَ** — nūn sākinah before **ت**, one of the fifteen → [[Ikhfa-Haqiqi]], hidden, 2
counts of hum. *anta* is read with the n never quite arriving.

Two nasal sounds in two words, both two counts, but the first is a **held doubled nūn** and
the second is a **hidden nūn**. If she can hear the difference between those, her ghunnah
work is finished.

### 9. عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ … وَسَتَّارُ الْعُيُوبِ … وَغَفَّارُ الذُّنُوبِ

Three parallel phrases, each with a doubled letter and an article. The parallelism is the
point: **same shape, three different lām decisions.**

| Phrase | Next letter | Sun or moon |
|---|---|---|
| الْغُيُوبِ | غ | **moon** — *al-ghuyūb* |
| الْعُيُوبِ | ع | **moon** — *al-ʿuyūb* |
| الذُّنُوبِ | ذ | **sun** — *adh-dhunūb* |

Also: **غ** in *ghaffār* and *ghuyūb* is elevated → **heavy**; **رُ** in *sattāru* and
*ghaffāru* carries a ḍamma → **heavy** ([[Ra-Tafkhim]]); and each of the three words has a
natural madd on the wāw, 2 counts, which is exactly where the fatigue shows.

### 10. وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ — [[Leen]], [[Tafkhim]]

**حَوْلَ** — wāw sākinah after a fatḥa: **līn**, flat, short. **قُوَّةَ** — qāf elevated →
**heavy**; doubled wāw, held.

### 11. إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ — the **light** [[Lam-Jalalah]]

**The point of the entire lesson.**

The preceding vowel is the **kasra** of *bi-* → the lām of the name of Allah is **light**
(muraqqaq). Thin, forward, said with the tongue high and front, nothing filling the back
of the mouth: *illā bi-llāhi*.

Compare with §1 in the same kalima: *astaghfiru-LLĀHa*, heavy after a ḍamma. **One
sentence, both forms.** Do not let this lesson end until she can produce them back to back
on demand.

### 12. الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ — the close

[[Lam-Qamariyyah]] twice, [[Tafkhim]], and [[Madd-Arid-Lissukun]] on the final word.

**الْعَلِيِّ** — ʿayn is a moon letter → l audible. Doubled yāʾ, held in the mouth — **no
ghunnah**, as established in 4-03 §4.

**الْعَظِيمِ** — ʿayn again, moon → l audible. **ظ** is elevated *and* has iṭbāq →
**heavy**, one of the two heaviest letters in the unit alongside the ṭāʾ of *khaṭaʾan*.

At the stop: the yāʾ is a madd letter and the mīm goes sākin → **madd ʿāriḍ lis-sukūn**, 2,
4 or 6 counts. *al-ʿaẓīīīm*. Choose a length and use the same one every time.

**In plain terms:** twice in this kalima a نْ turns into a meem before a ب, and both times it is the word *dhanb*. Find the two and the hardest part is done.

## Drills

**Drill 1 — the two lāms.** *astaghfiru-llāha* … *illā bi-llāhi.* Ten times, alternating.
Heavy, light, heavy, light. Then tell me the vowel that caused each. This is the drill for
this lesson and it outranks everything else here.

**Drill 2 — the rule-spotting pass.** Silent read, finger on each rule, name it aloud.
Mark the ones you cannot name.

**Drill 3 — lips closed.** *dhambin*, twenty times, watching your mouth in a mirror or
watching me watch you. **Your lips must meet.** If they do not meet, it is not iqlāb, it is
just a quiet n.

**Drill 4 — the trap pair.** *dhambin* … *adhnabtuhū*. First one: iqlāb, lips close. Second
one: no iqlāb, because the nūn has a fatḥa. Say them as a pair, twenty times, and tell me
each time which one has the rule.

**Drill 5 — the three treatments.** Say these three and name the rule for each:
> *min an* — the n out loud (iẓhār)
> *min kulli* — the n hidden, two counts of hum (ikhfāʾ)
> *dhambin* — the n turned into an m, lips closed (iqlāb)

Same nūn sākinah, three different fates. If you can sort these three by ear, you have the
nūn-sākinah family.

**Drill 6 — the heavy letters.** *khaṭaʾan* … *ghaffār* … *al-ʿaẓīm*. Fill your mouth on
each. Then say *sharīka* from 4-03 immediately after, thin, to feel the contrast.

**Drill 7 — the parallel three.** *ʿallāmu-l-ghuyūb, wa sattāru-l-ʿuyūb, wa
ghaffāru-dh-dhunūb.* Same rhythm three times. Two moon lāms then a sun lām. Do not let the
rhythm carry the third one into the wrong shape.

**Drill 8 — the breath plan.** Mark it first. The natural scheme is four breaths: after
*ʿalāniyataw wa atūbu ilayh* · after the first *aʿlamu* · after the second *lā aʿlam* ·
after *ghaffāru-dh-dhunūb* · then to the end. See [[Waqf-Types]].

**Drill 9 — the recitation pass.** Whole kalima, marked plan, half speed, then full. Then
name your own worst rule.

## Listen-for

**Listen for:** a thin *bi-llāh* at the end and a thick *astaghfiru-llāh* at the start, and
lips that visibly close on every iqlāb.

- **The mistake:** *illā bi-llāh* said heavy.
  **Why it happens:** four lessons of heaviness have built a habit, and this is the first
  contradiction. It is not that she does not know the rule — she can state it — it is that
  the name of Allah has one setting in her mouth.
  **Say:** *"Kasra before it. Thin. Smile and say it — bi-llāhi."* The smile forces the
  tongue forward and the heaviness cannot survive it.

- **The mistake:** *dhanbin* with an audible n.
  **Why it happens:** the nūn is written and there is no visible instruction to change it
  in most primers. Iqlāb is the least *visible* of the nūn rules.
  **Say:** *"Close your lips on it. Not n, m. dhambin."*

- **The mistake:** iqlāb applied to *adhnabtuhū* as well.
  **Why it happens:** over-generalisation from the word immediately before — she has
  spotted "n then b" and stopped checking. A good error: it means the rule has landed. It
  just needs its condition.
  **Say:** *"Look at the nūn. It has a fatḥa. The rule needs a sukūn. No iqlāb there."*

- **The mistake:** *min kulli* with the n fully pronounced.
  **Why it happens:** she has just spent 4-04 drilling three iẓhārs, and iẓhār is now the
  default.
  **Say:** *"Kāf is not a throat letter. Hide it — two counts in the nose, then kulli."*

- **The mistake:** the three parallel phrases flattened into one rhythm, the sun lām in
  *adh-dhunūb* pronounced as *al-dhunūb*.
  **Why it happens:** parallel structure creates rhythmic momentum, exactly as the list did
  in 4-04.
  **Say:** *"Third one is different. Shadda on the dhāl. Look before you say it."*

- **The mistake:** *al-ʿaẓīm* clipped at the end, madd ʿāriḍ cut to nothing.
  **Why it happens:** end of a long sentence, no air, and stopping feels like stopping
  early.
  **Say:** *"The last word is the longest. Four counts on the ī, then close."*

## Homework

1. Drill 1 — the two lāms — thirty times a day. Nothing else in this lesson matters as
   much.
2. Drill 4 — the *dhambin* / *adhnabtuhū* pair — twenty times a day, in front of a mirror.
3. The whole kalima once a day on your breath plan, at half speed.
4. Add today's stops to the rule card, and go back through 4-01 to 4-04 marking every
   occurrence of the name of Allah **H** or **L**. There should be no L before today.
5. Bring me the one rule you still cannot hear. Not the one you cannot do — the one you
   cannot *hear*. Those are different problems and they need different drills.
